* Automatic and adaptive tuning of parameters for iterative solvers based on Dynamic Relaxation (DR) or Pseudo-Transient schemes
* Ensures rapid and robust convergence for finite-difference and face-centered finite volume methods
* Examples for stokes flow, hydro-mechanical coupling, visco-elasto-viscoplastic shear banding and subduction
