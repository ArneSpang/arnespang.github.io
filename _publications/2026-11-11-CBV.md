* 3D thermo‐mechanical modeling integrates multi‐geophysical constraints to simulate the surface subsidence at Changbaishan volcano
* The decadal subsidence is most consistently reproduced by the gravitational settling of a dense melt core within a shallow mush reservoir
* Subsidence may reflect continued mechanical dynamism and internal mass redistribution rather than a waning magmatic system
